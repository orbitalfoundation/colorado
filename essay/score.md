# Essay score — written before the prose, checked after

Method: the emotional register is composed first (the simulate.world way),
the facts are orchestrated onto it. `heckler arc` verifies the trajectory
exists in the draft. Structure is the philosophy sandwich: thin bread of
philosophy top and bottom, meat in the middle, never woven through numbers.

| # | section | register | job |
|---|---------|----------|-----|
| 1 | The feed | distress, recognition | The claim as encountered in the wild; the feeling it produces; question volley naming it |
| 2 | What I am trying to do | first-person, calm | Top bread, short: testing worlds before living in them; why a model instead of an opinion |
| 3 | The ledger does not balance | clarity, relief | The structural fact; paper water vs wet water; nobody is stealing |
| 4 | Two cows and a denominator | wry, precise | Claims adjudicated: rangeland vs irrigated cow; 32 vs 46 percent; the alfalfa price correction |
| 5 | Why the obvious fix fails | sober | Buyout mechanics; Crowley County; permanence is not the same lever as a fallow year |
| 6 | The fight has a schedule now | urgency without panic | ROD Aug 21 2026; record-low Mead; every two years, forever |
| 7 | The instrument | agency, invitation | What the model is, what you can do with it, what it cannot do; failing to find a solution is the finding |
| 8 | Keeping futures open | hope, widening | Bottom bread: prior appropriation as fossil incentive; institutions as evolved artifacts; the ask |

Voice constraints (from heckler baseline): zero em-dashes; question volleys
are load-bearing; first-person only where true of Anselm (bracketed slots
where only he can speak); concrete numbers, period-labeled; no bows, no
exclamation marks, no "not just X but Y"; links inline, informally.
