# 2026-09-22 — naming note from the desk

Written by the desk session (computational-journalism), not the colorado
session; a note, not a change to this repo's site.

Anselm retitled the desk's card for this piece from "Who Drinks the
River?" to **"A call for leadership in the Colorado River Basin crisis"**,
with the rule: "the problem with clever but ambiguous naming is that the
site is already complicated; we have to make concepts simpler." He
pointed at the well-titled coverage the piece sits beside, for example
American Rivers, "The Ship Needs a Captain: A call for leadership in the
Colorado River Basin", Page Buono and Sinjin Eberle, 2026-03-18
(https://www.americanrivers.org/2026/03/the-ship-needs-a-captain-a-call-for-leadership-in-the-colorado-river-basin/),
and a general search for articles on Colorado River tensions.

For this repo: the story page's title, the `<title>`, the essay's title
and the share card should be reconsidered in the same spirit when the
tenor pass happens. Anselm also said today, of this piece, that it "does
not tell a tractable story of farms, choices, consequences"; the plain
title and that story are the same job. Not changed here.
